\caption{Five-lane SAC-QUTAB workflow. EuroSAT is partitioned by content group into 16,200 training, 4,050 validation, 2,700 calibration and 4,050 final-test images. ResNet-50 and ViT-Small/16 are trained with seeds 17, 29 and 43, producing six checkpoint candidates. Validation selects checkpoints and defines model-specific different-class control ECDFs. Calibration fits the nested failure detectors from calibration pairs, frozen checkpoints and ECDF-normalized representation displacement. The protocol is then frozen before evaluating nine non-sham doses and three identity shams per final-test source. Solid blue arrows carry image or paired-example data, dashed gray arrows carry metadata, and orange dash-dot arrows carry frozen artifacts or policy.}
\label{fig:framework}
