## Source hierarchy and exclusions

Protocol/indices and authenticated outputs precede derivative statistics, inspected implementation, proposal/reference material, and finally the old template. Method anchors are `src/sac_qutab/interventions.py`, `metrics.py`, `evaluate.py`, `detector.py`, `statistics.py`, `reporting.py`, resolved per-run configuration, and the frozen `model_contracts`. The report compares the *executed systems*, not architecture alone.

The previous reporting vocabulary repair is recorded in `A/software-fixes/report-contract-20260909_073309/repair-note.txt`. It is not the later stale representation-path defect. The original report index is not used as the authority for representation hashes. The audit checks the individual evaluation indices and independent statistics source records instead.

The auxiliary CKA and original attention images remain archived and are not headline evidence. Four qualitative pairs were reconstructed from authenticated real image/cache bytes, with their deterministic selection and recorded predictions checked. The attention overlay's coordinate defect is disclosed; its corrected version uses only one authenticated pair and one frozen-checkpoint forward call. JS–ECDF Spearman values in `relationships.json` are post-hoc descriptive correlations; correlated severity rows do not justify naive independent-row p-values. No new bootstrap run, semantic annotation, training, detector fitting or full-test inference was performed.

## Statistical checks and reporting units

- Clean metrics: one original probability row per source, not 12 repeated clean rows.
- Prediction/class tables: all sources; RQ3: only clean-correct, non-sham pairs.
- Means use all three seeds; SD uses denominator n−1. CI estimates remain distinct.
- AP is recall-increment weighted precision, not trapezoidal AUPRC.
- Primary Holm families contain one active model-pair contrast; the exploratory BH family contains 36 tests. Neither implies joint adjustment of every statement in this report.
- No exact-zero probability claim is made for stored p=0 values.
- Training throughput is excluded from architecture-efficiency claims because devices/software differ.
