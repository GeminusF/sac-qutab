# Contribution and review policy

Every planned publication PR has one owner and a different primary reviewer. The target is three PRs, six substantive commits and three primary reviews per member. Bootstrap/merge commits and corrective commits are reported separately. Counts do not replace scientific contributions.

The local automation reads `.github/pull_request_template.md` and `.github/review_template.md`. GitHub does not automatically load the latter into its review form. Review text contains executed checks, their exact SHA, failures and unchecked areas. Account switching and machine-generated reviews are disclosed in PUBLICATION.md; human review is not inferred from account activity.

Failed or missing required checks block approval. A changed head invalidates the review evidence. A clean CI run does not establish dataset licensing, semantic validity, historical authorship, or defense understanding.

The contribution assignment is a proposed equal publication responsibility allocation, not a retrospective statement of verified individual work. Final sign-off by all five members is recorded separately before release.
